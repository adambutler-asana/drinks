# Old Fashioned 🥃  
  
2 oz Maker’s Mark bourbon  
4 dashes Angostura bitters  
4 dashes orange bitters  
1/2 oz brown sugar simple syrup  
Orange peel  
Large ice cube  
  
1. Pour bitters and simple syrup in old fashioned glass  
2. Place large ice cube in glass  
3. Pour bourbon over the ice  
4. Express orange peel over glass and place the peel in the glass  
5. Stir for 20 seconds  
  
  
# Whiskey Sour 🍋  
  
2 oz bourbon  
1 3/4 oz lemon juice  
3/4 oz simple syrup  
1 egg white  
Angostura bitters  
  
1. Dry shake with all ingredients  
2. Shake with ice  
3. Strain over coupe glass  
4. Let it settle for a minute  
5. Several drops of bitters on top

# Clover Club 🍓

1/2 oz dry vermouth  
1/2 oz lemon juice  
1/2 oz simple syrup  
1 egg white

4. Double-strain into coupe glass (get the raspberry chunks out)

# Mojito 🌴  
  
2 oz white rum  
1 oz lime juice  
1/2 oz simple syrup   
Soda water  
2 fresh mint sprigs (8-10 leaves each)  
Lime wheel  
  
1. Clap mint sprig in hand to wake it up   
2. Gently press mint leaves with back of bar spoon in a tall glass to express oils into the glass   
3. Rub mint leaves/oil on inside of the glass  
4. Fill glass with ice to the top  
5. Pour rum, lime juice, and simple syrup into a shaker and shake with ice  
6. Strain into glass  
7. Top with soda water  
8. Use bar spoon to gently incorporate mint leaves up into the drink  
9. Garnish with fresh mint sprig and lime wheel  
  
  
# Cuban Pool Boy 🌴  
  
2 oz aged rum  
3/4 oz lime juice  
3/4 oz brown sugar simple syrup  
1 oz champagne  
1 oz egg whites  
Angostura bitters  
6 mint leaves  
Nutmeg  
Large cube  
  
1. Lightly muddle mint leaves in a shaker with simple syrup and lime juice  
2. Add in rum, egg whites, & bitters  
3. Shake with ice  
4. Double-strain into rocks glass to filter out mint fragments  
5. Top with champagne  
6. Garnish with sprinkle of nutmeg
